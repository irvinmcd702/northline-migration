Seal failures rarely happen without warning. Here is what our service techs look for first.

1.  Drips that grow week by week
2.  Crystals or residue around the gland
3.  A squeal at start-up
4.  Rising bearing temperature
5.  Vibration after a dry run

Catch any of these early and a seal kit is a one-hour job.
